The Fish Cutting Zone — Uploader

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel The Fish Cutting Zone (@fishcuttinghubtv).

What it does

This tool is used only by the owner of The Fish Cutting Zone to upload the channel's own short videos (AI-generated visualizations of traditional fish-cutting techniques) and to read the channel's own view statistics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Google and YouTube access

  • Upload videos to the owner's own channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Read the owner's own channel details and video statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API).
